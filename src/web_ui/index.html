Edit

Drag a point or a line to move it (a selection moves together). Click a line to add a point there. Click a point to select it; Range (or Shift-click) then selects everything up to the next point clicked, Ctrl-click adds one. Arrow keys move the selection 1 cm (Shift: 10 cm), Delete removes it. Drag anywhere else to pan. The original outline stays dashed.

Simplify

Drops every point that lies less than the tolerance off the line through its neighbours (like JOSM's Simplify way). Only the selected stretch when points are selected, else the whole outline.

What the mower learned by bumping (kept until Reset mission or a new mission from the beginning). A first bump isn't believed: the mower tries again (dashed), and if it drives through there it was a false detection (grey). Bumped again: confirmed (purple). The thin lines are the bumper at each touch; the filled cells are what is left after erasing everywhere the mower drove since - where the obstacle can be. Edge corrections: spots where the outline runs further in after perimeter bumps (dashed circles, kept across missions). Tap one on the map to forget it. Many corrections along one edge: re-record that boundary.
